Use your camera as one input for style and grooming ideas. The camera is optional, results are estimates, and you can edit every observation before recommendations are made.

Face-shape guide

These are visual style categories, not permanent labels. Real faces can sit between two shapes, so choose “Uncertain” or edit the estimate when needed.

Oval

Longer than wide with a softer, rounded jaw. Many styles can work.

Round

Similar length and width with fuller cheeks and softer angles.

Square

Similar length and width with a stronger, more angular jaw.

Rectangular

Clearly longer than wide, often with straighter side lines.

Heart

Wider forehead with a narrower chin area.

Diamond

Cheekbones appear widest while the forehead and jaw are narrower.
